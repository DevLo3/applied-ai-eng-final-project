# Exercise & Activity Plan — Woody

**Prepared by:** Dana Reyes, CPDT-KA — Portland Pawsitive Training Center  
**In consultation with:** Dr. Meredith Song, DVM  
**Date:** April 2025 (revised April 2026)  
**Patient:** Woody | Cavapoo | Male, Neutered | Age: 2 years

---

## Overview

Cavapoos are moderately energetic dogs with a Poodle's intelligence and a Cavalier's affectionate, laid-back streak. Woody requires consistent daily exercise for physical health and mental stimulation, but is not a high-drive working breed — quality of activity matters more than raw duration.

**Daily Exercise Target:** 45–60 minutes total (split across the day)  
**Mental Enrichment Target:** 15–20 minutes (separate from physical exercise)

---

## Daily Routine

### Morning — Primary Walk (25–30 min)
- **Time:** 7:15–7:45 AM
- **Format:** On-leash neighborhood walk with at least 5–10 min "sniff break" (loose leash, let Woody lead and sniff freely)
- **Goals:** Energy release, bladder/bowel routine, mental engagement through olfactory stimulation
- **Pace:** Moderate — not a jog. Woody sets the tempo during sniff segments.

### Midday — Short Relief Walk or Play (10–15 min)
- **Time:** 12:00–12:30 PM (if owner is home or dog walker available)
- **Format:** Backyard free play or short block walk
- **Goals:** Break up time alone, prevent restlessness
- **Note:** If midday outing is not possible, a frozen Kong or snuffle mat can substitute for mental engagement.

### Evening — Active Play + Training (20–25 min)
- **Time:** 6:00–6:30 PM
- **Format:** Off-leash play (fetch, tug) + 5–10 min training session
- **Goals:** Bond-building, skill reinforcement, final energy burn before evening rest
- **Caution:** Avoid vigorous exercise within 30 minutes of meals (bloat prevention, though low risk at Woody's size).

## Mental Enrichment Activities

Mental exercise tires Cavapoos as effectively as physical exercise. Aim for at least one enrichment activity daily:

| Activity | Equipment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Snuffle mat | Snuffle mat + kibble | Scatter ¼ of breakfast meal in mat |
| Frozen Kong | Kong Classic (size medium) | Fill with peanut butter (xylitol-free) + kibble, freeze overnight |
| Puzzle feeders | Nina Ottosson Dog Tornado (Level 2) | Use at breakfast 3x/week |
| Nose work (hide & seek) | Small treats | Hide 5–8 treats around a room; cue "find it" |
| Training session | Treats + clicker | 3–5 min focused sessions; practice known commands + 1 new behavior |
| Interactive play | Flirt pole or rope tug | 5–10 min; teach "drop it" before starting |

## Exercise Guidelines & Cautions

**Hot weather:** Do not walk on pavement when air temperature exceeds 85°F. Asphalt surface can reach 140°F+ and will burn Woody's paw pads. Walk in early morning or after sunset.

**Cold/wet weather:** Woody tolerates mild cold well due to his wavy coat, but limit outdoor time below 35°F to 20 minutes. Dry ears thoroughly after rain or swimming — moisture trapped in Cavapoo ear canals leads to yeast infections (see vet record, Feb 2026).

**Swimming:** Woody enjoys water. Supervised swimming is excellent low-impact exercise. Rinse and dry ears with veterinary ear cleaner (Epi-Otic) after every water exposure.

**Post-neuter restriction (Jan–Feb 2025):** Two weeks of strict rest following neuter surgery — no jumping, stairs, or running. Fully cleared for normal activity as of February 10, 2025.

**Joint health note:** Patellar luxation is a breed risk. Avoid repetitive high-impact jumping (e.g., jumping on/off furniture repeatedly). Ramps or steps recommended for couch/bed access long-term.

---

## Signs of Over-Exercise
- Excessive panting beyond 10 minutes after stopping
- Lagging behind or refusing to walk
- Limping or favoring a limb
- Stiff gait the morning after activity

If any of these appear, rest for 24 hours and contact the vet if limping persists.
