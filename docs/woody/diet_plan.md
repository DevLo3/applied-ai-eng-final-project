## Foods to Avoid

The following are toxic or harmful to dogs and must never be fed to Woody:

- **Grapes & raisins** — can cause acute kidney failure even in small amounts
- **Xylitol** (artificial sweetener found in gum, peanut butter, some yogurts) — causes rapid hypoglycemia
- **Chocolate, coffee, caffeine** — theobromine toxicity
- **Onions, garlic, chives** — damage red blood cells
- **Macadamia nuts** — neurological effects
- **Cooked bones** — splintering hazard
- **Avocado** — persin toxicity
- **Alcohol**
- **Raw yeast dough**

**Safe human foods (occasional, small amounts):**  
Cooked plain chicken or turkey, plain cooked rice, blueberries, carrots, cucumber, watermelon (seedless), plain cooked sweet potato, plain pumpkin puree (not pie filling).
