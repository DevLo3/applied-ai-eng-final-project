## Trainer Notes

> *"Woody is one of the most food-motivated Cavapoos I've worked with — he locks in quickly when treats are high value. He tends to disengage in noisy environments; short (3–5 min) sessions work better than single long ones. Clicker has been effective for shaping new behaviors. Recommend continuing trick work to keep him mentally engaged — this breed needs it."*  
> — Dana Reyes, CPDT-KA, November 2024

**Reward Preferences (ranked):**  
1. Freeze-dried chicken (highest value)  
2. Zukes Mini Naturals (salmon)  
3. Kibble (low value — use only for warm-up reps)

**Aversions / Sensitivities:**  
Startles at sudden loud noises (skateboards, metal clanging). Desensitization work ongoing.
