## Breed Overview — Cavapoo
A Cavapoo is a cross between a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and a Miniature or Toy Poodle. They are classified as a designer/hybrid breed and are not recognized by the AKC as a purebred.

**Typical Adult Size:** 12–25 lbs, 9–14 inches at the shoulder  
**Woody's Projected Adult Weight:** 18–20 lbs (based on parental weights)  
**Life Expectancy:** 12–15 years  
**Temperament:** Affectionate, social, gentle, highly trainable, low-shedding

## Known Breed Health Risks
Cavapoos can inherit conditions from either parent breed. Woody's owner should be aware of the following:

| Condition | Source Breed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mitral Valve Disease (MVD) | Cavalier | Leading cause of death in Cavaliers; screen annually after age 5 |
| Syringomyelia / Chiari Malformation | Cavalier | Neurological; watch for scratching at neck without contact |
| Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA) | Poodle / Cavalier | Inherited blindness; DNA-tested clear in both parents |
| Hip Dysplasia | Poodle | Both parents OFA-rated Good; low individual risk |
| Patellar Luxation | Poodle | Common in small breeds; annual orthopedic check recommended |
| Epilepsy | Poodle | Reported in Miniature Poodle lines; monitor for seizure activity |
| Sebaceous Adenitis | Poodle | Inflammatory skin condition; watch for scaly/patchy coat |
